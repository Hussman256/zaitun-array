The Temikoods story

Fabric with a memory, stitched into your everyday

Ankara isn't just fabric. It's celebration cloth — the print your aunt wore to the wedding, the pattern from your grandmother's wrapper, the colours of every owambe you've ever danced at.

Temikoods started with a simple frustration: the world's most joyful textiles were staying home while dull, mass-made bags travelled the world. So we changed that. We source genuine wax prints, cut each panel by hand to honour the pattern, and build bags tough enough for Lagos traffic, London rain and everything between.

Every Temikoods bag carries a story. Then it starts carrying yours.

— The Temikoods family
