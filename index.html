The Zaitun-Array story

Fabric with a memory, stitched into your everyday

Ankara isn't just fabric. It's celebration cloth — the print your aunt wore to the wedding, the pattern from your grandmother's wrapper, the colours of every owambe you've ever danced at.

Zaitun-Array started with a simple frustration: the world's most joyful textiles were staying home while dull, mass-made bags travelled the world. So we changed that. We source bold ankara, hand-dyed batik and textured weaves, cut each panel by hand to honour the pattern, and build bags tough enough for Lagos traffic, London rain and everything between.

Every Zaitun-Array bag carries a story. Then it starts carrying yours.

— The Zaitun-Array family

How your bag is born

Four hands-on stages.
Zero shortcuts.

01

The hunt

We scout Lagos markets for fabric with a story worth carrying — bold ankara, hand-dyed batik, textured weaves.

02

The cut

Each panel is placed and cut by hand so the boldest part of the print lands exactly where eyes do.

03

The build

Double-stitched seams, padded straps, lined interiors. Stitched by makers who sign off on every bag.

04

The send-off

Inspected, photographed, and confirmed with you on WhatsApp before it leaves our hands for yours.
