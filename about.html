Our story

The cloth spoke first.
We just gave it straps.

In every Nigerian home there is a wardrobe, and in that wardrobe there is cloth that remembers things. The gele your mother tied at your naming ceremony. The wrapper your grandmother wore to church for thirty years. Ankara is not fabric — it's a family archive printed in wax.

Temikoods began with a question that wouldn't leave us alone: why does cloth this alive stay folded in boxes while the world carries grey nylon?

Made slowly, on purpose

We are not a factory. Every Temikoods bag passes through human hands at every stage — fabric hunting in the market, hand-cutting each panel so the print lands boldly, double-stitching every seam, and a final inspection where someone personally decides your bag is good enough to carry our name.

We buy fabric in limited runs. That's a business decision and a love letter: it keeps every batch rare, and it means the bag on your back is one of a handful in the world.

Genuine wax prints, never cheap imitation. Construction that outlives trends. Personal confirmation of every single order on WhatsApp before it ships. And if a seam ever lets you down in the first six months, we repair or replace it — no forms, no drama.

Wherever you take a Temikoods bag, you're carrying more than your things. You're carrying proof that African craft belongs everywhere — boardrooms, lecture halls, airports, everywhere.
